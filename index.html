Club History

Founded 1878

Everton Football Club was founded as St. Domingo FC in 1878, making it one of the oldest football clubs in England.

The People's Club

Known as "The People's Club" and "The School of Science," Everton has a proud tradition of playing attractive, skillful football.

Goodison Park

Since 1892, Goodison Park has been home to Everton FC, one of the most historic stadiums in English football.

Legendary Players

Dixie Dean

Record holder with 60 league goals in a single season (1927-28). Everton's all-time top scorer with 349 goals.

Neville Southall

Legendary goalkeeper who made 751 appearances for the club. Winner of numerous individual awards.

Alan Ball

World Cup winner who became a midfield legend at Goodison Park during the 1960s and 1970s.

Duncan Ferguson

Fan favorite striker known for his physical presence and commitment. Now part of the coaching staff.

Historic Matches

1966 FA Cup Final

Everton defeated Sheffield Wednesday 3-2 at Wembley to claim the FA Cup.

1985 European Cup Winners' Cup

Everton beat Rapid Vienna 3-1 in Rotterdam to win their only European trophy.

1987 League Championship

The Toffees secured their ninth league title with a thrilling campaign.
